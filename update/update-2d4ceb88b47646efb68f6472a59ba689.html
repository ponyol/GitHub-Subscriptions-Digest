Since auth handling is entirely internal to the ORAS library, and the chunked upload requests are not naively rewindable, we have to indirectly fetch new tokens and manually supply them to the upload calls, suppressing ORAS's automatic retry functionality. We do this by making a request to the /v2/ endpoint and letting ORAS navigate the auth challenge.
