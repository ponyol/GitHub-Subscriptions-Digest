Release 0.5.2 introduces significant new features, including comprehensive translation capabilities with language detection and APIs, enhanced search responses, S3/file data download with streaming, expanded text chunking options, and usage telemetry. The update also brings improvements to the Notebook UX and configurable JWT lifetime.
