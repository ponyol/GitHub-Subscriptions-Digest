Signed-off-by: Riccardo Mancini <mancio@amazon.com> - fix(vsock): kick tx queue on restore Guest-to-host vsock connections could hang after restoring a snapshot. If a guest TX descriptor was available but unprocessed when the snapshot was taken (e.g. a connection-teardown packet), the restored TX queue had avail_idx ahead of the host-published avail_event. With VIRTIO_RING_F_EVENT_IDX the guest then treats notifications as suppressed and never kicks the TX queue again, so the host never processes the in-flight (or any later) descriptors and connections stall. On restore, proactively kick the TX queue so the device drains any in-flight descriptors and re-arms avail_event, instead of waiting for a guest notification that never comes. RX is left untouched: it is gated by pending_event_ack until the guest acks the transport reset, and the host pulls from the backend rather than waiting on a guest RX notification.
