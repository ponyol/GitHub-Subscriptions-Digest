Суммаризация (на русском)

This update introduces numerous new integrations for platforms like Jira, Slack, Reddit, Databricks, X (Twitter), Google Contacts, and many more, significantly expanding tool capabilities. A major focus was also on enhancing dropdown selectors, adding them for 14 integrations and improving their resolution and security. Workflow execution now supports nested loops and parallel blocks on the canvas, alongside critical memory leak fixes and performance improvements. Additionally, new GPT-5.4 models and a comprehensive API reference were added. --- Это обновление представляет множество новых интеграций для таких платформ, как Jira, Slack, Reddit, Databricks, X (Twitter), Google Contacts и многих других, значительно расширяя возможности инструментов.
