This update focuses on critical bug fixes to improve data integrity and concurrency. It resolves an `AssertionError` during concurrent table load/drop and corrects a catalog bug where view creation could lead to stale table versions. Further improvements include fixing expression retargeting to prevent "ghost columns" and enhancing testing reliability by reimplementing random operations statistics collection. --- Это обновление сосредоточено на критических исправлениях ошибок для улучшения целостности данных и конкурентности.
