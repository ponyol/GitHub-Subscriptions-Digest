The Mojo language evolves with the deprecation of `where` in parameter lists and the renaming of `package` to `precompile`, alongside new `stdlib` features like `LinkedList.index()` and `Optional.destroy_with`. MAX models benefit from streaming tool call support for Kimi K2.5, enhanced UTF-8 token handling, and all-FP8 KV-cache integration.
