The Cognee project introduced major enhancements to its self-improvement and retrieval mechanisms, including a new "agent" session context for learning from tool traces and "truth-subspace reranking" for integrating learned preferences into search results. The `README.md` documentation was significantly updated with a demo GIF and restructured content, improving user onboarding. Critical bug fixes were implemented for background pipeline execution, session lock management, and source provenance in search results, while Sentry and Langfuse observability integrations were removed. *** Проект Cognee получил значительные обновления, направленные на улучшение механизмов самообучения и извлечения информации.
