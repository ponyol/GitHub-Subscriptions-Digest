This update significantly expands platform capabilities with numerous new webhook triggers (e.g., Twilio, GitLab, PagerDuty, Zendesk) and full integrations for services like UptimeRobot, Downdetector, Thrive Learning, and Sakana AI, many featuring auto-registration. It also introduces a major overhaul of the rich markdown editor, adding @-mentions, inline image uploads, and improved streaming, complemented by comprehensive UI/UX enhancements across settings, access controls, and date pickers. Under the hood, key improvements include PII redaction via a Presidio sidecar, optimized secrets handling, and enhanced performance through React Query migrations, granular error boundaries, and refined database connection attribution. --- **Russian translation:** Это обновление значительно расширяет возможности платформы за счет добавления многочисленных новых вебхук-триггеров (например, Twilio, GitLab, PagerDuty, Zendesk) и полноценных интеграций с такими сервисами, как UptimeRobot, Downdetector, Thrive Learning и Sakana AI, многие из которых поддерживают автоматическую регистрацию.
