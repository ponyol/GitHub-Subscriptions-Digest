- Add cross-language portability results to cgo2027.tex The Threats to Validity paragraph claimed mechanism transfer to other transpiled dynamic languages without demonstrating it. That claim is now backed by three further existence-proof ports (CPython, Erlang/BEAM, Julia), each independently reimplementing qualification and rewrite against its own AST and loop construct, evaluated by the same two-pass corpus methodology as this paper's own Clojure study. New Discussion content (Section 6) presents the headline findings: mechanism transfer is confirmed, not argued; payoff is conditioned on whether the host already does allocation-elimination (real speedups in CPython/BEAM/FOL, near-1.0x or a regression in Julia, whose JIT already performs SROA); real-code incidence collapses toward zero in CPython and Julia specifically because both have rich mutate-via-method-call idioms, while BEAM (no mutation at all) qualifies far more of its real corpus; and a new three-way taxonomy of why a correctly-qualifying instance can still fail to pay off (host already did it, loop shape doesn't amortize the rewrite, escape point is mid-loop rather than at exit) drawn from BEAM's validate_headers/3 regression and Julia's Sockets.listenany zero-elimination result. Threats to Validity rewritten to retire the now-false claim and name the actual remaining gap: FOL's own Clojure proxy has no gate-faithful ASR-specific pass (only the syntactic upper bound is measured; the existing gate-faithful pass over that corpus measures a different mechanism, transient conversion), and BEAM's corpus is a single project family. New Appendix A reports full per-language corpora, methodology, and results (comparison table plus one subsection per language), including CPython benchmark numbers (2.95x-35.64x) measured directly since no prior source documented them.
